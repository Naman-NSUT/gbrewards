This page explains how to have your GB Rewards account and personal data deleted. You do not need the app installed, and you do not need to be signed in, to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to: kdfoamproducts@gmail.com
  2. Use this subject line:
    Delete my GB Rewards account
  3. In the message, include only your registered mobile number — the number you use to sign in to GB Rewards, in full international format.
    My registered mobile number is: +91XXXXXXXXXX
  4. Wait for our call or message on that number. We contact you on the registered mobile number to confirm the request really came from you. Once you confirm, we delete the account.
Never send us your password, OTP, or ID documents. Your mobile number is all we need to find your account. GB Rewards has no password, and no one from {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} will ever ask you for a one-time code.

How long it takes, and what you will receive

  • We aim to confirm we have received your request within 3 working days.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of us verifying the request.
  • We send you a confirmation, by email and to your registered mobile number, once the deletion is done.
  • If we cannot verify that the request came from the account owner, we will tell you why instead of deleting anything.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

Deletion removes everything that identifies you. It does not erase the anonymous business records of transactions that already happened, because we are required to keep those.

Your unredeemed points will be lost. Deleting your account permanently forfeits any points you have not yet redeemed, and cancels any redemption request that is still pending. This cannot be undone or reversed, and the points cannot be moved to another account. If you want to use your points, redeem them in the app first and wait for the redemption to be fulfilled — then request deletion.

Deleting is permanent

There is no way to recover a deleted account. If you use GB Rewards again later, you will need to register from scratch, and you will start at a zero points balance even if you sign up with the same mobile number.

Is there a delete button in the app?

No. GB Rewards does not currently have an in-app delete option — the email request described above is the way to delete your account, and it is the only way. Signing out of the app does not delete anything; it only removes the app's access on that phone.

Questions, or need help without the app?

Email kdfoamproducts@gmail.com for anything relating to your data — including asking for a copy of what we hold, correcting something that is wrong, or checking on a deletion request you already sent. You do not need an account, and you do not need the app, to contact us.
