Privacy Policy — GB Rewards

Android app in.gbrewards.gbrewards, published by {{LEGAL_ENTITY}}.
Last updated: 31 July 2026

GB Rewards is a rewards program app for authorised GoodBed dealers and channel partners. You scan the QR code printed on a product, the app awards you points, and you can request to redeem those points. This policy explains exactly what data the app collects, why, who receives it, and how to have it deleted.

It covers the GB Rewards Android app and the backend service the app talks to. It does not cover the separate web panel used internally by {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} staff.

2. What we do not collect

  • No photos or camera images. See section 3.
  • No location. The app has no location permission and never asks for one.
  • No contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS messages, or files from your device.
  • No financial or payment information. There is no payment, card, bank, or UPI field anywhere in the app, and points are not money.
  • No advertising ID, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. The app contains no third-party tracking software of any kind.
  • No crash or diagnostic reports. Crash reporting is not enabled in the published app.
  • No health or fitness data.

Your language choice (English or Hindi) is saved on your phone only. It is never sent to us.

3. The camera permission

The app asks for camera access for one reason: to read the QR code on a product. The camera image is decoded on your phone, in memory. No photo, video, or camera frame is ever saved to your device, saved to your gallery, or sent to our servers. The only thing that leaves your phone is the short code text printed inside the QR — which identifies the product, not you.

If you decline the camera permission, you can still sign in and use the rest of the app; you just cannot scan.

Other permissions listed on the Play Store page

The app's technical package also lists microphone, vibration, storage and display-overlay permissions. These are pulled in automatically by the camera and app-framework libraries we build on. GB Rewards does not use any of them — it contains no code that records audio, reads or writes your files, or draws over other apps. We list them here so that what you see on the Play Store permission list matches what we tell you.

4. Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies. No third party receives your data for its own purposes.

Your data is handled by two service providers who process it strictly on our instructions, under contract, and only to run the app:

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or to protect our legal rights or investigate fraud in the rewards program.

5. Technical information our server records

Like any internet service, our server briefly records the network (IP) address that a login request comes from. We use it for one purpose: to limit how many login codes can be requested from one place, so the system cannot be abused. It is stored for 24 hours and then automatically deleted. We do not use it to work out your location, and we do not link it to your profile.

6. Security

  • In transit: all communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • On your phone: your login tokens are held in the Android encrypted keystore, not in ordinary app storage. No personal details are cached on the device.
  • One-time codes are stored only as a cryptographic hash, expire after 5 minutes, and are limited to 5 attempts.
  • Access: only authorised {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} staff can see partner records, through a password-protected panel, and every change they make is recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take these measures seriously and review them.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account details (name, phone, address) and your points history for as long as your account exists and the rewards program runs, because your points balance is calculated from your full history and must stay auditable. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it as described in section 8, other than records we must keep for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons under {{JURISDICTION}} law — which we keep for no longer than {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}.

One-time login codes are deleted after 5 minutes. Login IP records are deleted after 24 hours.

8. Your rights — access, correction and deletion

You may, at any time and free of charge:

  • See what we hold about you. Your name, phone, address, balance, and full points history are visible in the app under Home and Profile. You can also ask us for a copy.
  • Correct it. You can edit your name and address yourself in the app's Profile tab at any time.
  • Have it deleted. See below.
  • Withdraw. You can stop using the app and request deletion at any time.

How to request deletion of your account and data

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the mobile number you registered with, or include that mobile number in the message, with the subject "Delete my GB Rewards account".

We will verify that the request is really from you, then delete your account and the personal data linked to it — your name, phone number, address, and your points and redemption history. We will complete this within 30 days and confirm by email.

Please note: deleting your account permanently forfeits any unredeemed points balance. If you want to redeem your points first, do that before requesting deletion. There is currently no delete button inside the app — deletion is handled by the request above.

9. Children

GB Rewards is a business tool for authorised dealers and channel partners. It is intended for adults aged 18 and over, is not directed at children, and is not designed to appeal to children. Sign-up requires a date of birth and the app refuses to register anyone under 18 — the check is enforced on our server, not just in the app. If you believe a minor has registered anyway, contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will delete the account.
