GB Rewards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoodBed (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the GB Rewards mobile application (the “App”). GB Rewards is a channel-incentive program for our authorised dealers and channel partners, who scan QR codes on genuine GoodBed products to earn and redeem reward points.

1. Information We Collect

DataWhy we collect it
Full nameTo create and identify your rewards account.
Mobile numberTo register you, send a one-time password (OTP) for login, and secure your account.
Postal / business addressTo identify your outlet and fulfil reward redemptions.
Reward activityProduct scans, points earned, and redemption requests — the core function of the App.
Diagnostics & crash dataAnonymous technical logs to detect and fix errors and keep the App stable.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or financial/payment card information.

2. Camera Permission

The App requests camera access solely to scan the QR code printed on GoodBed products. QR scanning is processed on your device; camera images are not stored, saved to your gallery, or transmitted to our servers. Only the decoded QR value is sent to verify and award points.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create your account and authenticate you via SMS OTP.
  • To award, track, and let you redeem reward points.
  • To process and communicate the status of redemption requests.
  • To detect fraud or misuse (e.g. duplicate or copied QR codes).
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data only with service providers that operate the App on our behalf:

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and rewards data for as long as your account is active and as needed to run the rewards program. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time (see Contact below); we will delete it unless we are required to retain records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the App.

7. Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords and OTPs, token-based authentication, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

8. Children

GB Rewards is intended for business channel partners and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above, and continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
